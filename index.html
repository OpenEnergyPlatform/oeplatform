    Open Energy Platform documentation#

    The Open Energy Platform (OEP) is an open research data infrastructure for energy system research. Researchers publish openly licensed data on it, from scenario projections to model inputs and results, described with FAIR metadata (OEMetadata) and annotated with the Open Energy Ontology (OEO), so others can find, compare and reuse it. Beside the database, the OEP hosts scenario bundles, which link a scenario's data, studies and models in the Open Energy Knowledge Graph, and factsheets that describe energy system models and frameworks. Everything is available in the browser and through a REST API. The OEP is the web interface of the Open Energy Family: openly developed tools, standards and an ontology, built by universities and research institutes.
