{% comment %} SPDX-FileCopyrightText: 2026 Jonas Huber © Reiner Lemoine Institut SPDX-License-Identifier: AGPL-3.0-or-later The datasets tab's action dialog, for a row (a batch of one): the contents of #dataset-action-body, from the Dataset action service's preflight (login.views.DatasetActionView). It states what the action does in words: - publish: where the Dataset will be listed (its Topics) and its member mix, as information only (draft and embargoed members stay hidden from others until their holders publish them, and never stop a publish). A draft failing the publish gate gets what it needs (``needs``) and no confirmation. A Dataset published already is passed over: the dashboard offers no republish. - unpublish: what leaves the catalogue, what others are told, what the scenario bundles citing it can no longer tell, and what stays. - delete: the member Tables are not deleted; a published Dataset also leaves the catalogue, its addresses answer 404 and citing bundles read the link as deleted, and its name has to be typed (``preflight.confirmation``), which the service checks under the lock. The form posts the eligible names only, as one comma-joined ``datasets`` field, so what runs is what was shown. A refused request comes back here with ``notice`` ("Nothing was changed: …") and the preflight run again; an unusable parameter comes back with ``errors`` beside its field. Links that open something else (a drawer, another dialog) carry ``data-close-then`` and ``hx-trigger="dialog-closed"``: dash_list.js closes this dialog first and sends their request once it has closed, because a modal cannot open a drawer or another modal while it is showing. The gate's "Edit…" (beside the missing Topic, for one Dataset: ``gate_dataset``) opens the Create and Edit form in this same dialog once it has closed, and hands focus back to the row's ⋯ (``data-action-origin``); "Manage tables…" arrives with the drawer it opens. {% endcomment %} {% with check=preflight n=preflight.eligible|length %}
