A share offers the user's own Organizations and the roles an Organization may be given (Data editor, Data maintainer); a removal offers the Organizations holding a role on one of the Tables. Each is shown with its member count, because every member gains or loses what it holds. Which Tables stay unchanged (a share: the role or more is held already) or are left out (a removal: it holds no role there, or its old Admin grant is the only Admin) depends on the choice, even for one Table, so choosing always asks the bulk preflight again. It is the Dataset dialog's re-check (table_action_dataset.html): #table-action-chooser receives the selects' ``change``, waits until the choice has rested and lets a newer request replace an older one, sends the whole selection as ``selection``, and swaps only the preview, the footer and the status line, never a select, which keeps focus. The preview carries what it was checked against (``previewed``), so a confirmation sent during the delay is checked again instead of run. {% endcomment %} {% if check.organizations %}
