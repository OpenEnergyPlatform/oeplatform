Every change posts here and the answer replaces these contents, so the drawer stays open while the list re-fetches behind it (``datasets-changed`` with ``stay``). What may be added and removed is the Dataset action service's to decide, and it decides again on every change; this only offers it. - the hand-off to the tables tab, filtered to this Dataset, when the user holds a role on any member (``own_count``): where several of their own members are published, shared or removed at once; - ``message``: what the last change did, or why it did nothing; - the gate line on a draft (``needs``); - every member, 25 per page, with a search over them where there are more than a page or a search is running. Remove is neutral and acts at once, except for a member the user could not add back (``row.lost``), whose Remove asks first (``ask_row``); - the add search (dataset_members_results.html). Every control has a stable id, so focus can be put back after a swap. The title carries what the page script reads after a swap: ``data-drawer-key`` (the Dataset, written into the address as ``?members=``), ``data-drawer-focus`` (the control to focus instead of the one that went, a Remove of a member removed) and ``data-announce`` (the message, said through the drawer's live region outside these contents). {% endcomment %} {% url 'login:dataset-members' profile_user.pk dataset.name as members_url %}
