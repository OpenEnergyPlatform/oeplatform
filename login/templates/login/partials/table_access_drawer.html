What the user may do is decided by the permission service (``login.table_roles``) and checked again on every write; this only offers it: - a Table admin changes or removes any Holder, adds a user or one of their own Organizations with a role in one step; - anyone else sees the list read-only, "Only Admins can change access", and the Table's Admins as small avatars (focusable, named on hover and focus); - anyone with a direct grant may leave the Table. ``confirm`` is the question the service asked (losing your own Admin, or the Table from your dashboard); ``pending`` the change it is about, posted again with ``confirm=yes``. ``notice`` is a refusal, ``errors`` maps a field to its error. ``gone`` means the Table has left the user's dashboard.
