By adopting a community-driven approach to peer review, we empower users to actively participate in maintaining high-quality standards, much like the collaborative model used by Wikipedia to safeguard the quality of its articles. Interested users can easily step into the role of a reviewer and scrutinize metadata submitted by data publishers. However, reviewing metadata is not a simple task. Prospective reviewers should have a solid understanding of the OEMetadata specification and be well-versed in the FAIR principles (Findability, Accessibility, Interoperability, and Reusability). This knowledge base ensures that reviews are both thorough and uphold the platform's commitment to data excellence.
