Open Energy Family Steering Committee What is the Steering Committee? The OEFamily-SC is responsible for the overall development and strategic direction of the Open Energy Family framework. The committee works closely with the core development team to ensure that the framework meets the needs of its users and remains aligned with the goals of the project. It meets regularly via conference calls and in-person meetings to exchange information and review planned and completed tasks. Who is involved? The Steering Committee is coordinated by the NFDI4energy project team.
