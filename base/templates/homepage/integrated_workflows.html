Integrated Workflows

Discover how combining multiple platform features creates powerful workflows for energy research and data management

Dataset Architect

Structures data records at the schema level. Defines tables, data types, and relationships as the foundation for consistent and validatable data management.

Data Importer

Transfers data to the OEP. Processes metadata, generates table structures, and enables efficient bulk data loading with validation

Metadata Wizard

Guides users through comprehensive metadata creation. Ensures compliance with OEMetadata standard and enhances data discoverability

Data Certifier

Validates data quality and assigns trust levels. Enables peer review and quality control workflows for published datasets

Data Archivar

Manages long-term data preservation and assigns DOI. Enables archival and citation of research datasets — under development
