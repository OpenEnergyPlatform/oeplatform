{# use: A section of a page's content starts with a plain h2 (a subsection with h3), no class; the spacing above and below comes with it, and a heading that opens its container gets no space above. #}

Sources and licences

Body text is 1rem. A section heading gets 2.5rem above and 0.75rem below, a subsection 1.75rem and 0.5rem.

Licence of the data

Headings take the heading colour and weight from tokens.

A minor heading

An h4 gets 1.25rem above; it is smaller than an h3.
