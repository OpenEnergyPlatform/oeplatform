Open Energy Ontology / OEO Viewer

The OEO Viewer serves as an interactive map for the Open Energy Ontology. While our Entity Lookup pages are used for quick lookups, this Viewer is built for experts to explore the structural context of the OEO—allowing you to visualize complex dependencies in the Graph View and navigate the full asserted hierarchy.
